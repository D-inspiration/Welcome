Software for informal commerce

Most business software is built for businesses with an ops team, a CRM budget, and reliable Wi-Fi. Atrivix builds for everyone else — the tailors, traders, and shop owners who run entire businesses from a phone, on hustle and relationships, not corporate infrastructure.

We started with tailoring because it's a sharp example of a bigger problem: real, profitable businesses tracked on paper notebooks and WhatsApp threads, losing orders and money to things that better software could quietly fix — without asking anyone to change how they already work.

Offline-first, always

Signal drops. Data plans run out. Our tools are built to keep working anyway, syncing when a connection shows up.

Built with real operators

We build alongside the tailors and shop owners actually using the product, not for a hypothetical enterprise buyer.

Small footprint, real impact

No sign-up walls, no bloated dashboards. Just the workflow compression that saves a few hours every week.

TixThreads is our first product. More tools for informal commerce — across other trades and verticals — are on the way.
