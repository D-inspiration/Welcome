Why We Built StitchFlow

Every tailor we talked to had the same problem: customer measurements scattered across notebooks, WhatsApp chats, and memory. One missed digit and a whole outfit doesn't fit.

The real cost of paper records

When a regular customer walks in for a new order, the fastest tailors still spend minutes flipping through old books to find their last measurements — if the book hasn't gone missing.

What StitchFlow does differently

  • Every customer's measurements saved once, searchable instantly
  • Order status tracked from "measuring" to "ready for pickup"
  • Works offline — no signal, no problem, syncs when you're back online

We built this for how tailoring businesses actually run, not how software usually assumes they run.
