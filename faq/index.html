Frequently asked questions

What is Atrivix?

Atrivix is a software company building tools for informal commerce — businesses that run on hustle and relationships rather than corporate infrastructure. TixThreads, for bespoke tailors, is our first product.

Does TixThreads work without internet access?

Yes. TixThreads is built offline-first, so you can take orders, log measurements, and manage clients with no signal. It syncs whenever a connection is available.

How much does TixThreads cost?

TixThreads is currently in early access, so pricing isn't public yet. Reach out and we'll fill you in on where things stand and how to get access.

Who is TixThreads for?

Bespoke tailors and similar made-to-order businesses that need to track client measurements, orders, and delivery status without the overhead of enterprise software.

Is my data safe?

See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's handled.

What's next after TixThreads?

We're planning to bring the same approach — offline-first, workflow-first — to other informal commerce trades. Follow the blog for updates.

How do I get in touch?

Email us any time — we read every message.
