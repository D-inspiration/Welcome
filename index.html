🔎 Fast Search

Find any customer by name or phone in under a second. Your entire customer base, instantly accessible.

No free-form text chaos. Every measurement is stored in a clean schema you can reuse, compare, and report on.

📅 Order Lifecycle

Each order moves through clear stages. See bottlenecks, manage workload, and never miss a deadline.
