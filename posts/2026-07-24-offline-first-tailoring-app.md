Power and connectivity aren't guaranteed for every shop, every day. Software that assumes constant internet access simply doesn't work for a lot of businesses.

## Offline-first, not offline-sometimes

StitchFlow caches your customer data and order pipeline directly on your device. Take a new order, update a measurement, mark something ready — none of it needs a live connection.

When you're back online, everything syncs automatically in the background. No manual refresh, no lost work.

That reliability is the whole point.
