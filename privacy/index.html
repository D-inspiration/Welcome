Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy covers atrivix.com and its product pages, including TixThreads. It explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Email address — if you subscribe to updates via the newsletter signup, or if you email us directly for support or early access.
  • Basic usage analytics — pages you visit, clicks, and session length, used to understand how the site is used. On our product pages, this data is stored locally in your own browser and is not transmitted to our servers.

What we don't collect

We don't sell your data, and we don't use third-party ad-tracking networks or cross-site tracking pixels on this site.

How we use it

  • To send occasional product and blog updates, if you've subscribed.
  • To respond to support requests and early access inquiries.
  • To understand which parts of the site and product are actually being used, so we can improve them.

Email service providers

Newsletter subscriptions are processed through a third-party email service provider. Your email is used solely to deliver the updates you signed up for.

Data retention

We keep subscriber emails until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Locally stored analytics data lives in your browser's storage and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser data.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any newsletter, or by contacting us.
  • Clear your browser's local storage to remove on-device analytics data.
  • Email us to ask what data we hold about you, or to request deletion.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, note it on the blog.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@atrivix.com.
