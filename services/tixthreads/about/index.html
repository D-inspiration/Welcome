The CRM that fits the way tailors work

TixThreads is client and order management built for bespoke tailors and made-to-order fashion businesses. It runs on a phone, keeps working when the signal drops, and is designed around the real rhythm of a tailoring shop: measurements, fittings, deadlines, deposits, and the customer who calls asking where their outfit is.

Most tailors we meet run a thriving business out of a notebook, a phone gallery, and WhatsApp threads. Orders slip, measurements get lost, and follow-ups are forgotten, not because the tailor is careless, but because nothing in their day was designed to catch it. TixThreads quietly does the catching.

What you can do with it

Customers & measurements

One searchable record per customer, with structured measurements you can reuse on every new order.

Order pipeline

Every order moves through clear stages with due dates, fabric notes, and one-tap status updates.

Needs-attention signals Pro

The signal engine flags orders and customers that need a follow-up, so nobody falls through the cracks. Nothing is ever messaged automatically.

Team & task delegation Pro

Assign work on an order to team members. Staff see their own "My Work" list; you see the whole workboard.

Invoices & receipts

Record payments and issue receipts against each order, so money owed is never a guess.

Reminders & alerts New

Push notifications for upcoming deadlines, plus email alerts for the things that matter.

Streaks & rewards New

Build a business-activity streak by doing the work that keeps a shop healthy. Rest days don't break it.

Online storefront & export

On Growth and above, get your own online storefront and export your data whenever you need it.

AskThreads New

Ask questions about your own business in plain language, by typing or by voice, and get answers from your own customers, orders and payments.

Camera text reader New

Snap a photo of a handwritten note or a customer's message, and the text lands in your question box for you to check before sending.

Works offline

Install it like an app and keep taking orders with no connection. It syncs when you're back online.

How we build

  • Offline-first. Signal drops and data runs out. The app is built to keep working anyway.
  • Your data stays yours. Every business gets its own isolated workspace. We don't sell data.
  • Built with real tailors. TixThreads is shaped by the businesses already using it, not by a hypothetical enterprise buyer.
  • Calm software. Fewer screens, fewer taps, and nothing that messages your customers without you deciding to.

Who is behind it

TixThreads is built by Atrivix, a Nigerian software company making tools for informal commerce, businesses that run on hustle and relationships rather than corporate infrastructure. TixThreads is our first product and the one we're investing in most heavily.

Where it's headed

We're working on deeper WhatsApp Business integration, visual garment templates in the style of a tech pack, and automated payment reconciliation. Follow the blog for updates as they ship.
