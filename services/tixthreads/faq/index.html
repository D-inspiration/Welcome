TixThreads FAQ

Answers about features, plans and your data. Can't find yours? Email us.

Getting started

What is TixThreads?

TixThreads is a CRM for bespoke tailors and made-to-order fashion businesses. It keeps customers, measurements, orders, payments and team tasks in one place, on your phone.

Who is it for?

Tailors and fashion businesses that take custom orders, whether you work alone or with a team of cutters, sewers and finishers.

Do I need to install anything?

No app store needed. Open TixThreads in your phone's browser and add it to your home screen. It then works like an installed app.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can take orders, log measurements and update statuses offline. Everything syncs when you reconnect. A banner shows when you're offline, and you can collapse it to a thin line.

Features

What are needs-attention signals?

Available on Pro, which is coming soon. The signal engine watches your orders and customers and flags what needs a follow-up, such as an overdue fitting or a customer you haven't heard from. It only shows you the list. It never messages anyone on its own.

How does task delegation work?

Available with staff accounts on Pro, which is coming soon. Add team members, then assign work on an order to them. Each person sees their own "My Work" list, and you see everything on the workboard.

What are streaks and rewards?

Doing meaningful business actions earns you a streak. You need active days in a week, not every day, so rest days don't break it. Milestones unlock rewards, and referring other tailors is one way to earn.

Can I issue receipts and invoices?

Yes. Record payments against an order and issue a receipt. Invoicing and receipts are included on every plan.

Will I get reminders?

Yes. TixThreads sends push notifications for upcoming deadlines and email alerts for important events.

What is AskThreads?

AskThreads is an assistant built into TixThreads. You ask a question in plain language, for example who owes you money or which orders are due this week, and it answers from your own business data. It is available only to the business owner and only sees your own workspace.

Can I talk to AskThreads instead of typing?

Yes. Tap the microphone, speak your question, and it is turned into text. You can read and edit it before you send.

What is the camera text reader?

Tap the camera button in AskThreads and take a photo of a handwritten note, a measurement list or a message. TixThreads reads the text and puts it in the question box so you can check it before sending. Photos are not saved. Each owner can read up to 20 photos per hour.

Does it work with WhatsApp?

WhatsApp reminders are included from the Starter plan. Deeper WhatsApp Business integration is in development, and we'll announce it on the blog when it ships.

Plans & payment

How much does it cost?

There is a free plan. Paid plans are Starter at ₦1,500, Growth at ₦5,000 and Pro at ₦10,000 (coming soon), each covering 30 days. See the pricing page for what each includes.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer. Submit your proof of payment in the app, we review it and activate your plan. You can pay for multiple months at once.

What happens if I hit my plan's limit or move down a plan?

Nothing is deleted. Records over the limit are locked until you upgrade. Delivered and cancelled orders always stay accessible and don't count toward your monthly order limit.

Can I buy Pro?

Not yet. Pro is coming soon. Email us or tap Notify me on the pricing page and we'll tell you when it opens.

Do you offer refunds?

If something went wrong with a payment, email us and we'll sort it out. See our Terms for details.

Your data & security

Is my customers' data private?

Yes. Every business has its own isolated workspace, and other businesses cannot see your customers, measurements or orders. We don't sell data. See the Privacy Policy.

What happens to my photos and voice when I use AskThreads?

Camera photos are shrunk on your phone, sent once to an AI service to read the text, and then discarded. We don't store the photo or log the text it reads. Voice is sent to an AI service to be turned into text. Your questions and the data needed to answer them are processed by Google's Gemini service. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I export or delete my data?

Data export is included on Growth and above. On any plan you can email support@atrivix.com and we'll help you export or delete your data.

What if I forget my password or can't log in?

Email us and we'll help you get back in.
