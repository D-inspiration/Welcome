How payment works

You pay by bank transfer. After you transfer, submit your proof of payment in the app and we review it and activate your plan. You can pay for more than one 30-day period at a time. Pro isn't open for purchase yet; tap Notify me and we'll tell you when it is.

Hitting a limit never deletes anything. Records over your plan's limit are locked, not removed, and unlock when you upgrade. Delivered and cancelled orders always stay accessible and don't count toward your monthly order limit.

Pricing questions

  • Is there a contract? No. Each plan covers 30 days and you pay for the periods you want.
  • What happens when I hit my limit? New records over the limit are locked until you upgrade. Nothing is deleted.
  • Can I move down a plan? Yes. Your data is kept, and anything beyond the lower plan's limits is locked until you upgrade again.
