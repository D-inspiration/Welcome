TixThreads Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how TixThreads, a product of Atrivix, handles information. It covers the TixThreads app and this website. For the main Atrivix site, see the Atrivix Privacy Policy.

Who is responsible for what

If you are a tailor or business using TixThreads, you decide what customer information to put in it. For that customer data, you are in control and we act as your service provider, storing and processing it on your behalf. For your own account details, we are responsible.

What we collect

  • Account details such as your name, email address, phone number and business name.
  • Business data you enter including customer names and phone numbers, measurements, orders, notes, invoices, receipts, team members and tasks.
  • Payment proofs that you upload when you pay for a plan by bank transfer.
  • Notification settings, such as the device token needed to send you push notifications if you turn them on.
  • Basic technical and usage information such as device type, and which features are used, so we can keep the service running and improve it.

How we use it

  • To run TixThreads and keep your workspace working, including offline sync.
  • To answer your questions in AskThreads and read text from photos when you ask it to.
  • To review payments and activate or manage your plan.
  • To send deadline reminders and alerts you have asked for, and service messages about your account.
  • To provide support and fix problems.
  • To understand how features are used and make them better.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or your customers' data.
  • We don't use customer data for advertising.
  • TixThreads never sends messages to your customers on its own. Anything customer-facing is something you choose to send.

AskThreads (AI assistant)

AskThreads lets the business owner ask questions about their own workspace, by typing, speaking or photographing text. To answer, we send your question, and the pieces of your business data needed to answer it, to Google's Gemini AI service. AskThreads is available only to the owner and only looks at that owner's own workspace.

  • Camera text reader: the photo is shrunk on your device, sent once to an AI vision service to read the text, and then discarded. We do not store the photo and we do not log the text it reads. The text is placed in your question box for you to review before you send anything. Photos must be JPEG, PNG or WebP and are limited in size and number per hour.
  • Voice: audio you speak is sent to an AI service to be turned into text. We do not keep recordings.
  • Our logs: if a feature fails, we log technical error reasons to fix it. These logs do not contain your photos, your API keys or the text read from photos.
  • Text inside a photo is treated as data to read, never as instructions to follow.

Please don't photograph or ask about information you don't have a right to use. How Google handles data is described in its own privacy terms.

Separation between businesses

Each business has its own isolated workspace. Other businesses on TixThreads cannot see your customers, measurements or orders. Only the people you add to your team can see your workspace, and what they can see depends on their role.

Service providers we use

We rely on trusted providers to run TixThreads: cloud hosting for the app and database, an email delivery provider for emails, browser push services for notifications, and Google's Gemini AI service for AskThreads (questions, voice and camera text reading). If we add payment processing or WhatsApp messaging, they will be covered here and you will see them in the app. These providers handle data only to provide their service to us.

Offline data on your device

To work offline, TixThreads stores a copy of your recent data on your device. Anyone with access to your unlocked device may be able to see it, so keep your phone secure and log out on shared devices.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your workspace data, except where we need to keep certain records, such as payment records, for legal or accounting reasons. Backups are removed on a regular cycle.

Your rights and choices

  • Ask what data we hold about you, or request a correction or deletion.
  • Ask for an export of your business data.
  • Turn off push notifications in your device or browser settings.
  • Ask us to close your account.

We aim to handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. If you have a concern we can't resolve, you may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Your customers' information

You are responsible for having a proper reason to store your customers' details and for using them respectfully. If one of your customers asks you to delete their record, you can do so in the app, or ask us to help.

Security

We use sensible technical and organisational measures to protect data, including access controls and separation between workspaces. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and keep your devices protected.

Children

TixThreads is built for businesses and is not intended for children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app or by email.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email support@atrivix.com.
